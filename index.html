Bubble Thoughts

Mood check-ins, as bubbles · for iPhone

A calm, private mood check-in. Every thought becomes a glowing soap bubble: its colour is your mood and its size is how strongly you feel it.

  • Check in in two seconds. Press and hold to blow a bubble, pick a colour, and add a few words only if you want to.
  • See your mindset over time. Replay the last 7 days, or play back a week, a month or a whole year on the timeline.
  • Something good every day. A gold positive-thought bubble from over a thousand original lines.
  • Private by design. Everything stays on your iPhone. No account, no cloud, no ads, no tracking. Optional Face ID lock.

Bubble Thoughts is free to download, with an optional one-time Bubble Thoughts Plus unlock. There is no subscription.

Bubble Thoughts is a wellbeing tool, not a medical device. If you are struggling, please reach out to someone you trust or a free helpline.
